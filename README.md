# Side Quest Journal

A calm daily journal with a twist: **one good thing a day**, plus little **side quests** for the days you're bored.

Try it: [https://autumnstarts.github.io/Little-knowledge/]

## Why I made it

I've kept a one-line journal for two years. Most people never get the habit because they don't know what to write. So this app asks for just one thing: *what's one good thing that happened today?* And when you're bored, it hands you a side quest instead of a blank page.

## What's inside

- **One good thing a day.** Pick any date and write it down. Your streak is shown on the card.
- **Side quests.** Get a random quest, choose from a list, or add your own. Finished quests are collected for you.
- **Yearly progress graph.** A month ruler (Experience / Opportunity), a rising curve, and a "You are here" marker. It's made for people who do quests only when they're bored.
- **Video diary (Reels).** Add short clips of your day and scroll through them.
- **Pattern lock.** Keeps people who pick up your phone out of your journal.
- **Your own photos.** Use your own pictures as card headers or page background.
- **Backup and restore.** Move your journal to another phone or browser.

## Privacy

- No accounts, no sign-up, no emails.
- Your entries, quests, photos and videos stay **on your own device**. Nothing you write is sent to a server.
- Because everything is stored in your browser, clearing browsing data deletes it. Use **Settings → Download backup** now and then.

## How it's built

A single `index.html` file (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript). No frameworks, no build step. Hosted free on GitHub Pages. Built entirely from a phone.
